About

DataUnlocked Labs is a personal research studio for building interactive explorations of complex systems.

The work centers on decision making under uncertainty, visual explanation, and making technical ideas legible to broader audiences.

Projects here are experiments: prototypes meant to provoke curiosity, test ideas, and invite closer looking rather than deliver polished products.
